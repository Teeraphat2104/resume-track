# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Single user (the developer). Uses this tool while actively job hunting — tracking applications, interview stages, and outcomes. Context is personal productivity: quick data entry, glanceable status, lightweight stats.

## Product Purpose

A personal resume submission tracker. Log every job you apply to, track pipeline status (sent → interviewing → rejected → offer → accepted), and get basic stats on your application funnel. Success means never losing track of where you are in any application.

## Brand Personality

Clean, professional, calm. No noise. No decoration for its own sake. Every element earns its place by serving the tracking workflow.

## Anti-references

- Not a kanban board or project management tool
- No gamification, no charts beyond what the stats page needs
- No heavy shadows, no glassmorphism, no gradient text
- No cards within cards

## Design Principles

1. **Fit the workflow.** The primary task is logging and checking status. Everything else is secondary. Keep the add/edit form fast; keep the dashboard glanceable.
2. **Calm hierarchy.** One level of information at a time. No competing visual weights on the same surface.
3. **Data clarity first.** Table rows, status badges, and filters are the core interaction. Make them precise before making them decorative.
4. **Personal scale.** This is a tool for one person. No multi-user abstractions, no permissions UI, no team features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA (4.5:1 body contrast, 3:1 large text)
- Reduced motion respected
- No color-only status indicators — badges use shape + label in addition to color
